# External resources and attribution

The study uses data from the following providers:
- CITYDATA.ai (mobile observations);
- NParks (park boundaries and facilities);
- Singapore Master Plan 2019 (land use);
- Singapore Land Authority / OneMap (routing);
- Singapore LTA (transport);
- OpenStreetMap (roads, and the outlines of the Chinese and Japanese Gardens);
- Google Maps Places API (some services).

Their records, routing responses and boundary or road datasets are not included. Obtain them from the providers under their terms. Providing a method does not grant data access.

The street-level imagery was self-collected. It is archived separately on Zenodo (DOI withheld during review), and no images or videos are distributed in this repository.

OneFormer and SPECS are externally developed models cited in the paper; their weights, training sets and configuration assets are not included. The OneFormer wrappers refer to `shi-labs/oneformer_ade20k_swin_large`.

Python dependencies keep their own licences, and none are vendored.
