# Data and code availability

## Statement in the manuscript (fill in the bracketed details after deposit)

The street-level imagery collected for this study has been deposited in Zenodo [DOI; withheld for double-anonymised review]. It will be made openly available upon publication under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) licence. The dataset contains 5,884 geotagged frames from Bishan-Ang Mo Kio Park, Jurong Lake Gardens and Punggol Waterway Park. Point files give each frame's GPS position and capture time. To protect privacy, human faces were masked and every frame showing a person who appears to be under 18 was removed. The original videos are not shared.

Analysis code, park-level descriptive summaries, model-level result tables and synthetic demonstration data are available at [repository URL], archived as version [release version] at [DOI]. Code is released under the MIT License, and the summary and synthetic data under CC BY 4.0.

The mobile GPS data were supplied by CITYDATA.ai and cannot be redistributed because of licensing and privacy restrictions. Raw locations and timestamps, device identifiers, trajectories, inferred residences, device-linked records and fine-scale mobility analysis inputs are not included. Access to the source mobile data must be arranged independently with the provider and is subject to its licensing and applicable privacy requirements. The authors cannot promise access or onward redistribution.

The public package supports inspection and plotting of the released results and running the model code with synthetic inputs. It does not allow exact independent reconstruction of all study findings, because re-estimating the study models requires the original authorised analysis inputs. Third-party spatial data (NParks, Singapore Master Plan 2019, OneMap, LTA, OpenStreetMap, Google Places) are available from their providers under their terms.
